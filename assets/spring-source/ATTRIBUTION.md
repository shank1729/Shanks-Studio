Local modifications: simple glasses, professional navy/white attire,
relaxed presentation pose, web material/geometry optimization, a lightweight
idle rig, and placement in the portfolio house.
Original hair, face, and proportions are to be retained.

The original source files are retained separately from customized outputs.

Lightweight edition: simplified cap/ponytail hairstyle, single navy top, plain
trousers, flat shoes, reduced geometry and smaller color textures. The detailed
customized edition is preserved separately as `assets/spring-detailed.blend` and
`assets/spring-detailed.glb`.

November 2026 appearance update: the two long side curls are removed, a parted
forehead fringe follows the user's drawn reference, and the irises are brown-black.
The lightweight edition's graphite spectacles are slightly larger and rounder.
